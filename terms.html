These terms explain how you can use iCheck and what you can expect from it. We've kept them short and in plain English. By using iCheck, you agree to them.

1. What iCheck does

iCheck looks for warning signs of a scam in a message, link, screenshot, phone call or phone number you check. It then suggests what to do next. iCheck is free and needs no account.

2. What iCheck can't do

  • It gives an assessment, not a guarantee. iCheck can miss a scam, and it can flag a genuine message. It can't confirm that a message, caller or website is genuine.
  • It doesn't replace your own checks. Before you pay, share personal details or call someone back, contact the organisation yourself, using its official app, website or the number on your card.
  • It isn't financial, legal or professional advice.
  • It is an independent app. It is not a government service. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any bank, telco, government agency or Scamwatch.

If you think you've been scammed, contact your bank straight away. Then contact IDCARE (1800 595 160) or report it to Scamwatch.

3. Using iCheck fairly

So that iCheck stays free and works for everyone, please don't:

  • try to get around the daily check limit, or use automated tools to send checks;
  • try to break, overload, copy or reverse-engineer the app or our servers;
  • use iCheck to build, test or improve scams; or
  • use iCheck for anything unlawful.

We may limit or block access that breaks these rules.

4. What you send us

Only check content you're allowed to share. Where you can, remove details you'd rather not send (such as your name or account number); the check still works without them. How we handle what you send is explained in our Privacy Policy: https://amanda1005.github.io/icheck-site/privacy.html.

5. Information from other sources

"Latest scams" shows alerts from Scamwatch (© Commonwealth of Australia, licensed under CC BY 3.0 AU), with links to the full alerts on the Scamwatch website. Official phone numbers and other facts come from the organisations' own websites, and we check them carefully. Websites and numbers can change, so always confirm them on the organisation's official website.

6. Changes to iCheck

We may change, pause or stop iCheck, or any part of it, at any time. We may update these terms. If a change is important, we'll let you know in the app.

7. Your rights under Australian law

Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or changes any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law or any other law that can't be excluded.

As far as the law allows, we aren't responsible for loss that comes from relying on an iCheck assessment instead of checking with the organisation yourself.
