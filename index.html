Check before you act

Got a text, email or link you're not sure about? Copy it and paste it into iCheck. You'll see the warning signs and what to do next.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

Ways to check

  • Check a message

    Copy the text, email or link. On Home, tap Check a message, paste it, then tap Check this message.

  • Share from Mail, Notes or Safari

    Select the text, tap Share in the menu, then iCheck. For a text message, press and hold it, tap Copy, then paste it into iCheck.

  • Check a screenshot

    Take a screenshot of the message, then tap Check a screenshot. The words are read on your phone; the picture is never sent.

  • Got a phone call?

    Tap Check a phone call and answer a few quick questions. You can also check whether a number is one an organisation publishes.

  • Latest scams

    See the latest scams Scamwatch is warning about, so you know what to look out for.

Our promises

  • We never say "safe"

    Scammers copy real messages, so no app can promise a message is genuine. When we don't find warning signs, we say so, and we still show you how to check with the organisation yourself.

  • No account. Your message isn't kept.

    No sign-up and no phone number. The text you check is analysed and not stored on our server. Screenshots are read on your phone. Your past checks stay on this phone.

Stop if they ask for any of these

  • Sending money, by bank transfer, PayID or crypto
  • A code sent to your phone
  • Your password, PIN or card details
  • Installing an app or letting them see your screen
  • Moving your money to keep it "safe"

Then contact the organisation yourself, using its official app, website or the number on your card. Real organisations won't mind.

Please note

  • iCheck gives an automated assessment to help you decide what to do next. It is not a guarantee.
  • It can be wrong. It can miss a scam, and it can flag a genuine message. It can't confirm that a message is genuine.
  • Before you pay, share details or call back, contact the organisation yourself, using its official app, website or the number on your card.
  • iCheck is an independent app. It is not a government service and is not affiliated with any bank, telco or government agency.
