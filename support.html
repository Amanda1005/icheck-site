Common questions

Does iCheck cost anything?

No. iCheck is free, with no ads and no account.

Does iCheck keep my messages?

No sign-up and no phone number. The text you check is analysed and not stored on our server. Screenshots are read on your phone. Your past checks stay on this phone.

Why doesn't iCheck ever say a message is safe?

Scammers copy real messages, so no app can promise a message is genuine. When we don't find warning signs, we say so, and we still show you how to check with the organisation yourself.

Is there a limit?

Each phone can check 20 messages a day. This keeps iCheck free for everyone.

Which languages does iCheck support?

The app can be used in English, 繁體中文, 简体中文 and Español. In this version, iCheck checks messages written in English.

I think a result is wrong. What should I do?

Tell us in the app ("Was this helpful?" after a result) or by email. If you're unsure about a message, contact the organisation yourself, using its official app, website or the number on your card.

iCheck is an independent app. It is not a government service and is not affiliated with any bank, telco or government agency.
